The experience

Amenities

A short list of things we do properly, rather than a long list of things we do adequately.

Wellness

The Spa

Four treatment rooms, including one built for two, looking directly onto the lagoon. Therapies run long by design — the shortest session on our menu is fifty minutes.

Open daily, 8:00 – 20:00. Guests may reserve up to 60 days ahead.

Water

The Pool Deck

An edgeless pool that appears to meet the lagoon at the horizon. Loungers are never double-booked — one per room, held for the length of your stay.

Open sunrise to 22:00, with a poolside menu served until 18:00.

Dining

The Dining Room

A short, seasonal menu built around what comes in from the lagoon each morning. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are all served with the same kitchen standard.

Reservations recommended for dinner; walk-ins welcome for breakfast and lunch.

Service

Concierge & Business

Airport transfers, dinner reservations across the city, and a small meeting room available for guests who bring work with them. Ask at the desk or over WhatsApp — whichever is faster for you.

Experience it for yourself.

Every amenity above is included for every guest — no resort fee, no fine print.
